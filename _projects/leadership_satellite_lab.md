**Satellite Communication Laboratory, Dept. of EEE, AUST — AUSTSAT Nanosatellite Program**

Co-initiated the establishment of the Satellite Research Lab at AUST and contributed to the AUST Nanosatellite Program (AUSTSAT), working on the nanosatellite and ground-station development effort within the Dept. of EEE.

At the 2023 Undergraduate Student Farewell, the Dept. of EEE recognized this work with an AUSTSAT appreciation crest, noting:

> "Your tenure in the Satellite Communication Laboratory at Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology (AUST) and your initiative contributions to developing the AUST nanosatellite and ground station have been nothing short of extraordinary. The dedication, innovation, and tireless efforts you've poured into these projects have pushed the boundaries of technology and set a high standard for excellence."
> — Prof. Dr. Tareq Aziz, Head of the Dept. of EEE, Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology
