System Architecture & Hardware Specifications

Agricultural automation requires long-range electrical noise immunity, extreme humidity resilience, and fail-safe power autonomy. The JRC AgriTech Board v3.0 serves as the central command node for commercial greenhouses and automated open-field irrigation projects across Bangladesh, managing multi-tier soil moisture/EC probes and high-current solenoid valves.

Subsystem Engineering Specification
Compute & Connectivity Espressif ESP32-WROOM module running FreeRTOS with simultaneous Wi-Fi mesh telemetry and local Bluetooth field diagnostics.
Fieldbus Physical Layer Galvanically isolated MAX485CSA+ RS485 differential transceiver running Modbus RTU over shielded twisted pairs spanning up to 1.2 kilometers across open farmland.
Arc-Free Solid-State Switching Dual ASSR-1218 photo-MOSFET solid-state relays providing 1500 Vrms galvanic isolation. Capable of switching AC/DC pump contactors and inductive solenoid valves without mechanical contact degradation or inductive kickback arcing.
Battery-Backed Power Tree USB Type-C power input → TP4056 lithium-ion single-cell charger → FP6291 high-efficiency step-up boost converter providing regulated 5V to field sensors and RS485 transceivers during grid outages.
I/O & Human-Machine Interface 20 broken-out GPIOs (ADC, DAC, PWM), I2C bus, auxiliary UART, and an onboard 0.96-inch monochrome I2C OLED display for live sensor telemetry and valve status indication.
