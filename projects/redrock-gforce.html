Redrock G-Force Module: Ultra-Compact Qi Wearable

Sub-miniature Flexible PCB Wearable Impact Sensor with Qi Wireless Charging for Traumatic Concussion Monitoring.

System Architecture & Hardware Specifications

The Redrock G-Force module was engineered to solve the rigorous spatial and power constraints of sports headgear (helmets, mouthguards) and patient fall monitoring. Designed on an ultra-compact polyimide flexible substrate, the module adheres directly to a miniature 24 mAh lithium-polymer cell, requiring zero physical connectors by utilizing sealed Qi inductive power transfer.

Subsystem Engineering Specification
Form Factor & Packaging 21 mm × 10 mm flexible polyimide substrate (V1/V2 revisions) laminated directly onto a 24 mAh LiPo pouch battery and sealed in IP67+ medical-grade silicone.
Microcontroller Subsystem STMicroelectronics STM32L0 ARM Cortex-M0+ ultra-low-power MCU operating in deep STOP mode (~12 µA quiescent draw).
Motion & Impact Sensing Analog Devices ADXL362 3-axis micropower MEMS accelerometer measuring configurable ranges from ±2g up to ±16g. Motion threshold events trigger asynchronous hardware interrupts over I2C to wake the MCU from STOP mode.
Impact Metric & Visual Feedback Continuous resultant acceleration vector computation: G = √(X² + Y² + Z²). 3-tier threshold LED feedback driven at 50% PWM: Green (<1.5g normal), Yellow (1.5–2.5g warning), and Red (≥2.5g severe impact alert).
Wireless Power Subsystem Wireless Power Consortium (WPC Qi v1.2) compliant inductive receiver (TI BQ51013B) feeding a 24 mA (~1C) linear charging circuit.
Battery Protection Integrated DW01A battery management IC with dual N-channel MOSFET switch preventing overcharge, over-discharge, and high-current short circuit events.
Target Applications Concussion and traumatic brain injury (TBI) tracking in contact sports, athletic mouthguards, military helmets, geriatric fall alerts, and industrial logistics shock monitoring.
