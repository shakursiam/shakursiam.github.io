Networkweigh: Industrial Ethernet Weighing Controller

System Architecture & Hardware Specifications

In harsh manufacturing plant environments, wireless networks suffer severe interference from motors, high-frequency inverters, and heavy metallic structures. The Networkweigh controller addresses these factory realities by combining a hardwired Ethernet TCP/IP interface with an analog front-end capable of resolving microvolt strain gauge signals.

Subsystem Engineering Specification
Hardwired Determinism WIZnet W5500 SPI-to-Ethernet controller with hardware-integrated TCP/IP protocol stack, providing noise-immune, zero-packet-drop communication independent of factory RF congestion.
Analog Signal Conditioning Precision, high common-mode rejection ratio (CMRR > 110 dB) instrumentation amplifier front-end with precision low-temperature-coefficient resistor network to amplify sub-millivolt Wheatstone bridge outputs from industrial load cells.
Host Processor Subsystem Espressif ESP32-WROOM-32U running real-time digital filtering (moving average, outlier suppression) and tare/calibration algorithms.
Isolated I/O Expansion Microchip MCP23017T 16-bit I2C I/O expander controlling relay drivers, industrial warning tower lights, limit switches, pneumatic diverter gates, and operator keypad matrix.
Chassis & Protection 4-layer rigid PCB engineered with dedicated analog and digital ground splits (star-grounded at power inlet) and transient voltage suppression (TVS) arrays on all field-facing terminals.
