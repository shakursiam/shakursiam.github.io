**AUST Innovation & Design Club (IDC)**
**Aug 2020 – Oct 2023 · 3 yrs 3 mos**

Joined AUST IDC as a Sub-Executive Member and built the club's official website, then advanced through the club's ranks to Vice President — the club's second-highest student leadership position — over three years.

- Joined as a Sub-Executive Member and developed the official AUST IDC website.
- Organized the Robo Race Competition at MindSparks 2022, a national tech-business competition.
- Promoted to Executive Member and appointed Head of the Innovator Team, leading hardware project development within the AUST IDC project team.
- Elevated to Vice President (Fall 2022), taking on a leadership role guiding the club's strategic direction — advancing from Web Developer to VP over the club's ranks.
- Managed 90+ volunteers and directed major technical initiatives, including VLSI career seminars.
- Served as Organizing Head of MindSparks'23, AUST IDC's flagship national tech-business festival (presented by One Bank Limited) — successfully managing 1,200+ participants from across the country and raising BDT 1.2M in funding through strategic marketing, corporate outreach, and event planning.
