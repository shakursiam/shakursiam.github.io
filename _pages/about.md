I am an Electronics & Embedded Systems Engineer and Research Assistant at the [HIVE (Hydrogen, Intelligent Vehicle & Energy) Research Group](/), Dept. of EEE, [Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET)](http://www.buet.ac.bd/), working under **Dr. Muhammad Anisuzzaman Talukder**. I also serve as a Research Associate (Consultancy) at the **Infrastructure Investment Facilitation Company (IIFC)**, contributing to a national scientific inventory assessment report for the Ministry of Finance.

My interest in embedded systems started in my third year at Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology (AUST), when a classmate and I formed a small student team, AUST Rocketry, to compete in the model rocket category at Teknofest-23. As Avionics Sub-system Lead under Prof. Dr. Omar Farrok, I worked with microcontrollers, sensors, actuators, and control systems for the first time — and that project led directly into my undergraduate thesis on the flight control system of a subsonic sounding rocket for meteorological particle observation, where I also learned to design PCBs. I carried that hardware foundation into industry at Frontech Limited, building the JRC AgriTech Board, a custom controller for farm machinery that senses soil moisture in real time.

At BUET, I've drawn on that hardware background for battery health prognosis: I proposed and built a charger that charges a battery and predicts its health at the same time, designed around a BQ24610 buck charger and an ESP32-S3 controller. I trained a lightweight State-of-Health / Remaining-Useful-Life model across the NASA, Oxford, MIT, and CALCE battery datasets, tested it on chemistries it had never seen, then quantized the best-performing model and deployed it directly on the charger itself — validated on two commercial cells over 14 charge cycles with under 2.4% prediction error (manuscript in preparation, Elsevier *Journal of Energy Storage*). Alongside this, I led a review of 168 papers on security and privacy in AI-based IoT systems, and I currently research sound-source localization for a government-backed traffic-horn detection system. I've also worked remotely as a hardware/embedded engineer for companies in the UK and Germany — one of the devices I helped design is now sold commercially in the UK (see [Projects](/projects/)).

This combination of hardware design, machine learning, and systems thinking is what draws me toward my intended research direction: **edge AI, in-sensor computing, cyber-physical systems, and wearable IoT devices** — building full sensing-and-inference pipelines that fit onto a single low-power board, small and efficient enough to live on the body, in the field, or inside a battery.

I completed my B.Sc. in EEE at AUST in 2023, where I also served as Vice President of the AUST Innovation & Design Club — managing 90+ volunteers — and co-initiated the university's Satellite Research Lab (see [Projects](/projects/) for both).
