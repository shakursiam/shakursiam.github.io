Teaching, Technical Workshops & STEM Outreach

Workshop Instructor — Hands-on PCB Design for Embedded Applications
February 26, 2025
Organizer: AUST Innovation and Design Club (AUSTIDC) • Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology

I had a fantastic time conducting the ‘Hands-on PCB Design for Embedded Applications’ workshop on February 26, 2025, organized by AUST Innovation and Design Club! It was a pleasure sharing insights into PCB design, its importance in embedded systems, and hands-on KiCad 8.0 training with circuit enthusiasts. While mastering PCB design takes continuous practice, this session served as a starting point for participants to step into the world of hardware development.

Software: KiCad 8.0 Host: AUSTIDC Focus: Embedded Systems & PCB Layout Audience: Circuit Enthusiasts & Students
Curriculum & Practical Training Delivered:
  • Importance of Custom PCBs: Illustrated why purpose-built circuit boards are vital for signal integrity, noise immunity, and power efficiency in wearable and IoT cyber-physical systems.
  • Hands-on KiCad 8.0 Workflow: Led students through schematic capture, library symbol mapping, footprint assignment, board stack-up, trace routing, and Design Rule Checks (DRC).
  • Design for Manufacturing (DFM): Demystified Gerber export, drill file generation, and real-world considerations when submitting designs to fabrication houses.
