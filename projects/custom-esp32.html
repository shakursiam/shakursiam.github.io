System Architecture & Hardware Specifications

Generic commercial ESP32 DevKit boards frequently suffer sudden brownout resets when transmitting high-power Wi-Fi/BLE packets due to poor capacitor placement and weak LDO transient response. To support sounding rocket field ground stations and static rocket motor test stands, this custom development board was engineered with dedicated power filtering and reliable USB-UART switching.

Subsystem Engineering Specification
Core Wireless MCU Espressif ESP32-WROOM-32 module integrating dual 32-bit Xtensa LX6 cores, 448 KB ROM, 520 KB SRAM, and Wi-Fi 802.11 b/g/n + Bluetooth 4.2 BR/EDR/BLE.
USB-to-UART Bridge Silicon Labs CP2102 single-chip USB-to-UART bridge offering rock-solid driver support across Linux, macOS, and Windows with baud rates up to 2 Mbps.
Transient Decoupling Network Multi-tiered parallel ceramic capacitor arrays (10 µF bulk tantalum + 1 µF + 100 nF high-frequency ceramic 0603s) placed directly adjacent to MCU power pins to absorb rapid 400 mA current surges during RF transmission bursts.
Deterministic Auto-Reset Logic Dual NPN transistor circuit driven by DTR and RTS modem control lines, providing glitch-free automated entry into UART download bootloader mode from PlatformIO and ESP-IDF without holding manual buttons.
Power Regulation Thermal-optimized linear regulator (AMS1117-3.3) with wide copper pour heat sinking, capable of delivering sustained 1A peak current from 5V USB feeds.
